# Orbit development update — major additions since Build 238

Development source/local builds through Build 265 / v0.3.244. Public tester downloads remain Build 238; these notes do not announce a newer public package.

- **Acid Bass:** native mono bass synth with saw/square waveforms and dedicated Accent and Slide controls in the sequencer.
- **Expanded pattern editing:** group move/copy, selection tools, individual note velocities, scale snapping, ghost notes and additional phrase-editing tools.
- **Playlist editing:** group selection, moving/copying arrangements and transferring complete patterns between generators.
- **Recording:** metronome count-in and audible MIDI overdubbing while patterns loop.
- **Song automation:** dedicated Event Editor alongside pattern automation, with recording/editing for instrument, effect and mixer parameters.
- **Mixer and routing:** expanded buses, returns, output routing, sidechains and channel/EQ settings copy and paste.
- **VST3 effects:** effects browser, insert-chain editing and settings/chain copy and paste.
- **LFO automation tool:** shared Pattern/Song curve generation with live visual preview, tempo sync, waveform, phase, fades and curvature.
- **Independent built-in generators:** Acid Bass, Drum Rack and 4OSC controls can stay open together inside the workspace.
- **Native distortion:** original Tube and Two-Band effects, usable on Acid Bass and other tracks.
- **Native dynamics:** lookahead limiter and original clean digital Peak Compressor.
- **Experimental Orion SFS recovery:** generator selection, tentative pattern/arrangement recovery and missing-audio relinking. Original instrument/effect sounds are not restored.

Work continues through Orion's manual section by section. The Event Editor now stays inside the workspace and has loop-range editing. These are development additions; the public tester package remains Build 238. Pro-9-style drum synthesis is the next requested generator and is not included yet.
