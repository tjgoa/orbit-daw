Labyrinth Home
Drag a knob vertically. Use arrow keys for small changes, Home/End for its limits, or double-click to reset. Values appear while hovering or adjusting.
Every frame comes from the included, compiled JUCE C++ renderer. No CSS/canvas knob redraws. Sizes 32/48/64 px use matching 1× or 2× native images; other screen/browser scaling can resample. 128 value positions per style.
